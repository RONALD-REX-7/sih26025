# Subsurface Mechanical Enclosure & Mounting Specification

## 1. Environmental Challenges in Underground Coal Mines

Underground coal seams in India (e.g., Jharia, Raniganj, Singareni) present hostile physical environments:
* **Relative Humidity:** 85% to 98% condensing humidity.
* **Corrosive Acidic Mine Drainage:** Water pH typically 4.5 to 6.2 with high sulfate and chloride content.
* **Suspended Coal Dust & Rockdust:** Abrasive particulate ingress.
* **Mechanical Blasting Shocks:** Blasting accelerations up to 15g peak.
* **Roof Sagging & Ground Convergence:** Tensile strain on cables and anchor points.

## 3. Rockbolt Mounting Bracket Assembly

The node must mount rigidly to underground colliery strata supports without slipping during continuous mining or depillaring operations:
* **Bracket Material:** 3.0 mm thick Grade 304 Stainless Steel plate.
* **Mounting Method:** Clamps directly over standard 22 mm or 24 mm ribbed tor-steel rockbolts (rebar roof bolts) or friction stabilizers.
* **Fastening:** Dual high-tensile M10 zinc-plated grade 8.8 bolts with split-spring locking washers.
* **Vibration Decoupling:** 2.0 mm thick high-density ethylene propylene diene monomer (EPDM) dampening pad between the mounting plate and enclosure backplate to isolate drill vibration while transmitting genuine bulk ground tilt.
