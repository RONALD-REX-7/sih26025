# SIH26025 — Technical Standards, Regulations & Research References

## 1. Statutory Regulations & DGMS Circulars

1. **Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS)**
   * *The Coal Mines Regulations (CMR), 2017.*
     * **Regulation 112:** “Strata Control and Monitoring Plan (SCAMP)” — Mandates real-time roof monitoring, support verification, and shift handover sign-offs in underground coal workings.
     * **Regulation 114:** “Powers and Duties of Safety Officers and Strata Management Officers.”
     * **Regulation 181:** “Use of Electrical Apparatus in Gassy Coal Mines.”
   * *DGMS (Tech) Circular No. 04 of 2017:* “Guidelines for Strata Monitoring in Mechanised Longwall and Continuous Miner Workings.”
   * *DGMS (Tech) Circular No. 06 of 2020:* “Approval of Electronic Equipment, Wireless Sensor Networks, and Environmental Telemetry in Underground Coal Mines.”

2. **Ministry of Coal, Government of India**
   * *Smart India Hackathon 2026 Problem Statement Definition (SIH26025):* “Development of an AI-enabled Low Cost Real Time Mine Subsidence Monitoring, Prediction and Early Warning System for Underground Coal Mines in India.”

## 2. Technical Standards & Testing Norms (BIS & IEC)

1. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**
   * **IS/IEC 60079-0:2017:** *Explosive Atmospheres — Part 0: Equipment — General requirements.*
   * **IS/IEC 60079-11:2011:** *Explosive Atmospheres — Part 11: Equipment protection by intrinsic safety 'i'.*
   * **IS/IEC 60529:2001 (Reaffirmed 2019):** *Degrees of protection provided by enclosures (IP Code) — IP67 / IP68.*
   * **IS 5780:2002:** *Specification for Intrinsically Safe Electrical Apparatus and Associated Circuits for Use in Explosive Atmospheres.*

2. **Wireless & Telecommunications Standards**
   * **DoT / WPC (Wireless Planning & Coordination Wing, India):**
     * *National Frequency Allocation Plan (NFAP-2022):* Sub-GHz License-Free Industrial Band (**865 – 867 MHz**). Maximum Effective Isotropic Radiated Power (EIRP): 1 W (30 dBm).
   * **LoRa Alliance:**
     * *LoRaWAN Regional Parameters v1.0.3 (IN865-867 Band Specification).*
